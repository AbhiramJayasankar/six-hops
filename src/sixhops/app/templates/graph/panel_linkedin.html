Import LinkedIn connections

Add everyone you're connected to on LinkedIn, with their current company and title. You'll review every change before anything is saved, and importing the same file again changes nothing.

  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings, then Data privacy, then Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose Connections and request the archive. LinkedIn emails you a link, usually within minutes.
  3. Unzip it and upload Connections.csv here.
